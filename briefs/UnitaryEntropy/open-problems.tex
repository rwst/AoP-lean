\noindent
Open problems raised by the technical brief in this directory, and by its Lean formalization
(see \lean{formalization-log.tex}).

\section*{From the brief}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Classical counterpart.} State and prove the classical analogue: Hamiltonian
  evolution is equivalent to preservation of the Shannon entropy and of ``the definitional
  independence of degrees of freedom''. (Brief, Sec.~4: ``This parallels the classical case
  where Hamiltonian evolution is equivalent to the preservation of Shannon entropy (Liouville's
  theorem)''.)
\item \textbf{Entropy conservation as determinism and reversibility.} The brief reads DR-INFO as
  ``an information theoretic way to characterize determinism and reversibility''. A precise
  definition of deterministic and reversible evolution, and a proof that it is equivalent to
  DR-INFO, are not given. (Brief, Sec.~1.)
\item \textbf{Propagator for time-dependent $H(t)$.} ``The Schr\"odinger equation gives rise to a
  differentiable unitary propagator $U_{t_0\to t_1}$'' is assumed. The formalization proves
  existence for constant $H$ only; for bounded $H(t)$ it is the linear ODE existence theorem,
  and for unbounded self-adjoint $H(t)$ it needs Kato-type hypotheses. (Brief, Sec.~3, proof of
  Theorem~2 ($\Rightarrow$); Log, ``Differences from the brief''.)
\end{enumerate}

\section*{From the formalization}
\noindent Beyond finite dimension the formalization has a part in any dimension; it leaves
open the items ``Unbounded Hamiltonians'', ``Norm-differentiable $U(t)$ in infinite
dimension'' and ``Isometric semigroup without a past'' below.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Unbounded Hamiltonians.} Step~3 assumes $U_{t_0\to t_0+dt} = I + A(t_0)dt + o(dt)$,
  and EVDIFF on every density operator forces a bounded $H$ (Banach--Steinhaus). The right form
  of EVDIFF for unbounded $H(t)$, differentiable only on a dense set of states, and the
  corresponding version of Theorem~2 are open here. (Log, ``Problems found in the brief'', Step~3
  item and last item of the any-dimension part.)
\item \textbf{Mixed states.} $\Phi_{t_0\to t_1}(\rho) = U_{t_0\to t_1}\rho\,U_{t_0\to t_1}^\dagger$
  for mixed $\rho$ is not formalized. It needs $\Phi$ affine (Kadison); whether preservation of
  equal mixtures alone suffices is not settled. (Log, ``Differences from the brief'', last item
  of the finite-dimensional part.)
\item \textbf{Equation at every time.} The formalization gives $U(t)$ only for $t$ near $t_0$ and
  the Schr\"odinger equation only at $t_0$. A global differentiable unitary propagator
  $U_{t_0\to t_1}$ for all times, with phases chosen consistently with
  $\Phi_{t_1\to t_2}\circ\Phi_{t_0\to t_1} = \Phi_{t_0\to t_2}$, is not built. (Log,
  ``Differences from the brief'', item on \lean{schrodinger_of_preserves}.)
\item \textbf{Norm-differentiable $U(t)$ in infinite dimension.} In any dimension only the strong
  equation $\psi'(t_0) = (i\hbar)^{-1}H\psi(t_0)$ is proved; the phase-fixing basis sum does not
  converge in operator norm. Whether a phase choice makes $t\mapsto U(t)$ differentiable in
  operator norm is open. (Log, any-dimension part, ``Differences from the brief'', EVDIFF item.)
\item \textbf{Isometric semigroup without a past.} The right shifts $V_t$ on $L^2(0,\infty)$ give
  an evolution that satisfies [B]EVMIX and preserves the entropy but is induced by no unitary;
  this example, and the Wold--Cooper reduction of every such semigroup to it, are not
  formalized. (Log, any-dimension part, ``Problems found in the brief'', second item.)
\end{enumerate}
