\paragraph{Differences from the brief.}
\begin{itemize}
\item The wave functions are handled through their densities $|\psi|^2 = e^{-x^2}/\sqrt\pi$ and
  $|\phi|^2 = 1/(\pi(1+y^2))$. ``In the domain of the position operator'' is read as
  $\int x^2|\psi|^2 < \infty$, i.e.\ $x\psi \in L^2$.
\item The frame change is checked as a strictly increasing coordinate change satisfying
  eq.~(2) in both forms. The unitary $U$ between the two $L^2$ spaces and the change of
  variables in the integral are not formalized.
\item Mathlib has no error function; \lean{Real.erf} is defined in \lean{ForMathlib}.
\item {[B]}STDIST is proved for $n, m \ge 1$ through the classification of separable
  infinite-dimensional Hilbert spaces, which is also new in \lean{ForMathlib}.
\item The additional remarks of Section~4 are not formalized.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Problems found in the brief.}
\begin{itemize}
\item The brief says that $\phi(y)$ ``goes to zero at infinity like $1/y^2$''. It is $|\phi|^2$
  that decays like $1/y^2$; $\phi$ itself decays like $1/|y|$. The conclusion is unaffected:
  $y\,\phi(y) \not\in L^2$.
\item ``$L^2(\mathbb{R}^n)$ is a separable Hilbert space for any $n$'' is true, but the
  isomorphism argument also needs infinite dimension, which fails for $n = 0$.
\end{itemize}
