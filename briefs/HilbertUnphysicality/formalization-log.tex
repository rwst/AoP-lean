with three physical conditions. The brief proves four mathematical propositions (Sec.~2) and
then argues each part of Theorem~5 from one of them (Sec.~4). The propositions are checked in
Lean; Theorem~5 is checked by reading, against them.

\paragraph{Differences from the brief.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{Proposition~1.} Both parts are stated for linear maps, without Mathlib's partially
  defined operators. Discontinuity is proved for an unbounded linear map on any normed
  space, which includes $\mathcal{D}(A)$ with the norm inherited from $\mathcal{H}$.
  Hellinger--Toeplitz is proved for an everywhere-defined symmetric map; self-adjointness is
  not needed.
\item \emph{Proposition~2.} The number operator is not formalized as an unbounded
  self-adjoint operator. Its expectation is \lean{numberExp}~$x = \sum_n n\,|x_n|^2$ on all of
  $\ell^2$ (the sum is $0$ where it diverges), which agrees with $E_N$ on normalized vectors of
  $\mathcal{D}(N)$. The sequence $x_j$ is the brief's.
\item \emph{Proposition~3.} The wave functions are handled through their densities
  $|\psi|^2 = e^{-x^2}/\sqrt\pi$ and $|\phi|^2 = 1/(\pi(1+y^2))$. ``In the domain of the
  position operator'' is read as $\int x^2|\psi|^2 < \infty$, i.e.\ $x\psi \in L^2$. That $f$ is
  strictly increasing is proved; that it is smooth and onto $\mathbb{R}$ is not. The unitary
  $U$, its inverse $V$, the change of variables, and the domain of $UXU^{-1}$ are not
  formalized. Mathlib has no error function; \lean{Real.erf} is defined in \lean{ForMathlib}.
\item \emph{Proposition~4.} The classification of separable infinite-dimensional Hilbert spaces
  is new in \lean{ForMathlib}. The positive integers $n, m$ appear as $n + 1, m + 1$.
\item \emph{Theorem~5.} Not stated in Lean; checked by reading. Each part is a direct
  application of a proposition, for one particular system: the oscillator (Proposition~2),
  one spatial degree of freedom (Proposition~3), and two systems with different numbers of
  continuous degrees of freedom (Proposition~4). That a unitary carries rays, density
  operators, self-adjoint operators, the Born rule and the entropy is not formalized. Neither
  are the closing remarks on classical cutoffs and on Schwartz spaces.
\item The additional remarks of Section~5 are not formalized.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Problems found in the brief.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Cross-references lack their label: the proof of Theorem~5 has ``This satisfies the
  conditions for 2'', ``As shown in 3'' and ``By 4'' for Propositions~2, 3 and~4.
\item ``Schwartz spaces present the same problem'' is asserted without proof, and without
  saying which structure on $\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^n)$ is meant (see the open problems).
\item QSTO:H still allows any separable Hilbert space, finite-dimensional ones included. This
  no longer affects Theorem~5, whose proof reads the inconsistency as the failure of a
  condition for one particular system, but the brief could say so.
\end{itemize}
