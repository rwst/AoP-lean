\noindent
Open problems raised by the technical brief in this directory, and by its Lean formalization
(see \lean{formalization-log.tex}).
Items marked \textsc{[blocks verification]} stand in the way of checking a main
claim of the brief as the brief states it: a \emph{formalization gap} can be closed
in Lean alone, while a \emph{brief issue} needs the brief to be corrected or made
precise first.

\section*{From the brief}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Further problems.} The list of ways in which QSTO:H fails the base conditions is
  open-ended: ``If the reader is aware of other problems that should be added, do contact us.''
  (Brief, Sec.~1, footnote~1.)
\item \textbf{A physically adequate model.} The brief concludes that ``the solution is not to
  find workarounds for these issues, but to fix the mathematical model to better reflect the
  physical requirements''. A replacement for QSTO:H that satisfies [B]COBS, [B]WDQ and
  [B]STDIST is not given here; one candidate is the brief ``Physical vector spaces for quantum
  mechanics''. (Brief, Sec.~6.)
\item \textbf{Schwartz spaces.} ``This third failure is not related to the problems of
  continuity, as Schwartz spaces present the same problem.'' Presumably this means that
  $\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^n)$ and $\mathcal{S}(\mathbb{R}^m)$ are isomorphic as topological
  vector spaces, as both are isomorphic to the space $s$ of rapidly decreasing sequences
  through the Hermite functions. This is not shown, and it is not said which structure the
  isomorphism has to carry. (Brief, Sec.~4, proof of Theorem~5, last paragraph.)
\item \textbf{Classical cutoffs.} For [B]COBS and [B]WDQ the brief says that in classical
  mechanics ``one can, in principle, put a cutoff on all variables and work only with joint
  probability distributions in that compact region'', which ``bound all moments'', while
  ``Quantum mechanics does not allow that''. Neither half is made precise. For the quantum half,
  one reading is that a nonzero wave function and its Fourier transform cannot both have
  compact support. (Brief, Sec.~4, proof of Theorem~5.)
\end{enumerate}

\section*{From the formalization}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{The Schr\"odinger equation with a potential.} The remark that the
  Schr\"odinger equation holds only on $\mathcal{D}(H)$ is checked for every self-adjoint
  Hamiltonian $H$ that is unbounded on its domain, with the evolution $e^{-itH/\hbar}$ of
  Stone's theorem (\lean{schrodinger_only_on_domain}). It is checked in detail for $H = N$ on
  $\ell^2(\mathbb{N})$, for $H_0 = P^2/2$ on $L^2(\mathbb{R})$, and for the harmonic oscillator
  $P^2/2 + X^2/2$ on $L^2(\mathbb{R})$, built from the Hermite basis
  (\lean{oscillator_schrodinger_only_on_domain}). For other potentials $V \ne 0$, it is not
  formalized that $H = P^2/2m + V(x)$ is self-adjoint, nor on which domain. This needs results
  such as the Kato--Rellich theorem, which Mathlib, TauCeti and Physlib do not have. For the
  oscillator, that its Hamiltonian is the sum $H_0 + X^2/2$ of partially defined operators is
  not proved either. (Brief, Sec.~5; Log, ``Differences''.)
\end{enumerate}

\noindent
The blocker of the previous version of the brief, that the conditions and the inconsistency
were informal, is resolved. The revised brief adds a minimum form to each condition (Sec.~3)
and reads the inconsistency as the failure of a condition for one particular system
(Sec.~4). The previous open problem on the expectations of a general unbounded observable is
no longer raised: the [B]COBS part now rests on the number operator alone (Proposition~2).
The formalization gap ``Theorem~5 itself'' is closed: Theorem~5 is stated and proved in Lean
as \lean{theorem5}, in the readings of the next section. No open problem now blocks
verification.

\section*{Precise readings}

The revised brief confirms the readings proposed for its previous version, with the changes
below. They are the readings in which Theorem~5 is stated in Lean (\lean{theorem5}).

\paragraph{Inconsistency.} ``QSTO:H is inconsistent with C'' means: there is a quantum system
to which QSTO:H applies whose QSTO:H model fails condition C. The systems are the
one-dimensional harmonic oscillator for [B]COBS, a system with one spatial degree of freedom
for [B]WDQ, and two systems with different numbers of continuous degrees of freedom for
[B]STDIST. Under this reading finite-dimensional Hilbert spaces do not need to be excluded.

\paragraph{[B]COBS.}
\begin{itemize}
\item \emph{States} are the nonzero vectors of $\mathcal{H} = \ell^2(\mathbb{N}_0)$, with the
  norm topology; this is equivalent to the quotient topology on $P(\mathcal{H})$, since
  everything below is invariant under $\psi \mapsto c\psi$.
\item The observable is the number operator $N$ with domain $\mathcal{D}(N)$ of
  Proposition~2.
\item The \emph{expectation} of $N$ is $E_N(x) = \braket{x|Nx}/\|x\|^2$, defined for
  $x \in \mathcal{D}(N)\setminus\{0\}$.
\item [B]COBS, minimum form: $E_N$ is continuous on $\mathcal{D}(N)\setminus\{0\}$ with the
  subspace topology.
\item Claim to be proved: $E_N$ is not continuous at $e_0$ within
  $\mathcal{D}(N)\setminus\{0\}$ (Proposition~2; \lean{expN_not_continuousWithinAt} and
  \lean{not_continuousOn_expN}, checked). That $N$ is self-adjoint on $\mathcal{D}(N)$
  and that $E_N(x) = \braket{x|Nx}/\|x\|^2$ there are checked too
  (\lean{isSelfAdjoint_numberOp}, \lean{coe_domain_numberOp}, \lean{expN_eq_inner}).
\item Not claimed by the brief, but true: for every densely defined, symmetric, unbounded $O$,
  $\psi \mapsto \langle O\rangle_\psi$ is discontinuous at every point of
  $\mathcal{D}(O)\setminus\{0\}$.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{[B]WDQ.}
\begin{itemize}
\item A \emph{change of frame} is a smooth increasing bijection $y = f(x)$ of $\mathbb{R}$. It
  acts on states by the unitary $U\colon L^2(\mathbb{R}, dx) \to L^2(\mathbb{R}, dy)$,
  $(U\psi)(y) = \psi(g(y))\sqrt{g'(y)}$ with $g = f^{-1}$ (Proposition~3).
\item Each observer measures position with the multiplication operator by their own
  coordinate, on its maximal domain: $X$ on $L^2(\mathbb{R}, dx)$ and $Y$ on
  $L^2(\mathbb{R}, dy)$. The brief now says this, and notes that $UXU^{-1}$ is the different
  operator of multiplication by $g(y)$, with domain $U\,\mathcal{D}(X)$. It is checked
  that $X$ and $Y$ are self-adjoint (\lean{isSelfAdjoint_positionOp}), that $UXU^{-1}$ is
  multiplication by $g(y)$ (\lean{conjLinearPMap_frameUnitary_positionOp}) and self-adjoint
  (\lean{isSelfAdjoint_conjLinearPMap_frameUnitary_positionOp}), and that $UXU^{-1} \ne Y$
  (\lean{conjLinearPMap_frameUnitary_positionOp_ne}).
\item A normalized state has \emph{finite variance} for an observable $O$ if it lies in
  $\mathcal{D}(O)$, that is, if $\|O\psi\|^2 < \infty$ (Sec.~4).
\item [B]WDQ, minimum form: for every change of frame and every state $\psi$,
  $\psi \in \mathcal{D}(X)$ implies $U\psi \in \mathcal{D}(Y)$.
\item Claim to be proved: for $f(x) = \tan(\tfrac{\pi}{2}\operatorname{erf}(x))$ and
  $\psi = (e^{-x^2}/\sqrt\pi)^{1/2}$, $\psi \in \mathcal{D}(X)$ but
  $U\psi = (1/(\pi(1+y^2)))^{1/2} \notin \mathcal{D}(Y)$ (Proposition~3;
  \lean{gaussState_mem_positionDomain}, \lean{frameUnitary_gaussState},
  \lean{frameUnitary_gaussState_not_mem} and \lean{not_forall_frameUnitary_mem_positionDomain},
  checked).
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{[B]STDIST.}
\begin{itemize}
\item The \emph{systems} are those with $n \ge 1$ continuous degrees of freedom
  (Proposition~4 is stated for positive integers).
\item The \emph{mathematical representation} of a system is
  $\mathcal{H}_n = L^2(\mathbb{R}^n)$ with what QSTO:H builds from it: $P(\mathcal{H}_n)$,
  $D(\mathcal{H}_n)$, the self-adjoint operators, the Born rule and the von Neumann entropy.
  The brief now lists exactly these, so the positions $X_1, \dots, X_n$ and momenta
  $P_1, \dots, P_n$ are not part of the representation.
\item Two systems have the \emph{same} representation if a unitary
  $\mathcal{H}_n \to \mathcal{H}_m$ carries one onto the other.
\item [B]STDIST, minimum form: for $n \ne m$ there is no unitary
  $\mathcal{H}_n \to \mathcal{H}_m$.
\item Claim to be proved: for all $n, m \ge 1$ there is one (Proposition~4;
  \lean{L2_euclidean_iso}), and it carries the QSTO:H structure (\lean{carriesQSTO},
  \lean{exists_carriesQSTO}). Both are checked.
\end{itemize}
