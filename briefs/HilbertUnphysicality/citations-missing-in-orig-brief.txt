Brief: Unphysicality of Hilbert spaces for quantum mechanics
Compiled 2026-11-14 from the literature search in ~/physics/plans/report-hilbert.html.

The brief has no reference list. Each entry below gives the work, the place in the brief it
bears on, what it says, and how well this was checked:
  [read]    the statement was read in the paper (the passage named, not the whole paper);
  [agent]   read by a search agent only, not re-read;
  [bib]     existence and bibliographic data confirmed, content not read.

A. THE SOURCE AND ITS PUBLISHED DISCUSSION

1. G. Carcassi, F. Calderón, C. A. Aidala, The unphysicality of Hilbert spaces, Quantum Stud.:
   Math. Found. 12, 13 (2025), arXiv:2308.06669. [agent] (in Lean)
   Bears on: the whole brief. The paper behind it. Offers Schwartz space as a "much more
   reasonable candidate", "not a final and general answer".
   PDF: CarcassiCalderonAidala2023-2308.06669.pdf

2. G. Carcassi, T. Thrien, C. A. Aidala, Reverse physics for quantum mechanics,
   arXiv:2608.27543. [agent] (in Lean)
   Bears on: Sec. 3-4. Source of [B]COBS, [B]WDQ, [B]STDIST and QSTO:H; its Theorem 3 is the
   brief's Theorem 5, with the same frame change.
   PDF: CarcassiThrienAidala2026-2608.27543.pdf

3. N. A. Lemos, Are Hilbert spaces unphysical? Hardly, my dear!, Found. Phys. 55, 46 (2025),
   arXiv:2501.03294. [agent] (in Lean)
   Bears on: Theorem 5, all three parts; Sec. 6. The main rebuttal. WDQ: the change of
   variables is a change of basis. STDIST: a system is Hilbert space plus Hamiltonian. Against
   Schwartz space: X^3 P + P X^3 has no self-adjoint extension.
   PDF: Lemos2025-2501.03294.pdf

4. G. Carcassi, R. Rozite, C. A. Aidala, Response to "Are Hilbert spaces unphysical? Hardly,
   my dear!", arXiv:2507.09738. [agent] (in Lean)
   Bears on: Theorem 5, [B]WDQ. Coordinate transformation versus change of basis.
   PDF: CarcassiRoziteAidala2025-2507.09738.pdf

5. Z. Lu, Is the existence of unbounded operators a problem for quantum mechanics?,
   arXiv:2603.10601; Unbounded operators, physicality, and modality in quantum theories,
   arXiv:2609.28136. [agent] (first one in Lean)
   Bears on: Sec. 1, Sec. 6. Any restriction to "physical" states is arbitrary; compact
   support is not preserved by free evolution; Schwartz space excludes meaningful evolutions.
   PDF: Lu2026-2603.10601.pdf, Lu2026-2609.28136.pdf

B. PRIORITY FOR THE THREE FAILURES

6. J. E. Roberts, The Dirac bra and ket formalism, J. Math. Phys. 7 (1966) 1097-1104. [read,
   Sec. 1-4] (in Lean)
   Bears on: Theorem 5, [B]STDIST; Proposition 4. States the failure in 1966: with all rays
   as states and all self-adjoint operators as observables, "the mathematical structures
   assigned to these quantum systems are mutually isomorphic. Yet we can clearly distinguish
   between the different physical systems". His fix: labeled Hilbert spaces.
   PDF: roberts1966dirac.pdf

7. A. Heathcote, Unbounded operators and the incompleteness of quantum mechanics, Philos.
   Sci. 57 (1990) 523-534. [read in part]
   Bears on: Proposition 1; Theorem 5, [B]COBS; Sec. 6. A unit vector outside D(Q) has no
   position expectation. He argues against restricting the state space: domains of different
   observables do not coincide, self-adjointness is lost, and the lattice of propositions
   (Mackey, Gleason) is disturbed. (in Lean)
   PDF: heathcote1990.pdf

8. R. Werner, Physical uniformities on the state space of nonrelativistic quantum mechanics,
   Found. Phys. 13 (1983) 859-881. [read in part]
   Bears on: [B]COBS, "small changes in state". Which uniformity on states is physical, for
   bounded observables: "the usual quantum mechanical formalism contains no physical uniformity
   on the state space". Answers with a coarser topology and a larger state space.
   PDF: werner1983.pdf

9. F. Gieres, Mathematical surprises and Dirac's formalism in quantum mechanics, Rep. Prog.
   Phys. 63 (2000) 1893, arXiv:quant-ph/9907069. [agent] (in Lean)
   Bears on: Sec. 5 (commutators, domain of the Schrödinger equation). A catalogue of the
   contradictions that come from ignoring domains.
   PDF: Gieres2000-quant-ph9907069.pdf

10. K. Schmüdgen, Unbounded Self-adjoint Operators on Hilbert Space, GTM 265 (2012),
    Proposition 10.1; B. Simon, Tosio Kato's work on non-relativistic quantum mechanics,
    arXiv:1711.00528, Theorem 10.14 (Davies-Kato). [agent]
    Bears on: Proposition 2. The expectation of a semibounded operator is lower
    semicontinuous, so the jump in Proposition 2 can only go up.
    PDF: Schmüdgen-Book.pdf, Simon2018-1711.00528.pdf

C. FOR "SCHWARTZ SPACES PRESENT THE SAME PROBLEM" (last sentence of the proof of Theorem 5)

11. R. Meise, D. Vogt, Introduction to Functional Analysis, Oxford 1997, 14.9 and Example
    29.5(2). [bib; cited through Langenbruch 2006, who was read] (in Lean)
    The Schwartz space of R^n is isomorphic to the sequence space s for every n.

12. M. Langenbruch, Hermite functions and weighted spaces of generalized functions,
    Manuscripta Math. 119 (2006) 269-285, Example 5.3. [read in part] (in Lean)
    Contrast: the Gelfand-Shilov spaces S^r_r(R^n), r > 1/2, are isomorphic to duals of power
    series spaces whose exponent sequence k^{1/(2rn)} depends on n. With the classification
    in D. Vogt, Lectures on Fréchet spaces (2000), Thm 5.12 [agent], they are pairwise
    non-isomorphic for different n. The case r = 1/2 is not covered by a formal statement.
    PDF: langenbruch2005.pdf, Vogt2000.pdf

D. FOR THE CLASSICAL-CUTOFF REMARKS IN THE PROOF OF THEOREM 5

13. G. B. Folland, A. Sitaram, The uncertainty principle: a mathematical survey, J. Fourier
    Anal. Appl. 3 (1997) 207-238, Theorem 7.2 (Benedicks 1985; Amrein-Berthier 1977). [agent]
    (in Lean)
    "Quantum mechanics does not allow that": no nonzero function is supported, together with
    its Fourier transform, on sets of finite measure.
    PDF: Folland1997.pdf

E. FOR SEC. 6, "FIX THE MATHEMATICAL MODEL": CANDIDATES IN PRINT

14. J. E. Roberts, Rigged Hilbert spaces in quantum mechanics, Commun. Math. Phys. 3 (1966)
    98-119; J.-P. Antoine, J. Math. Phys. 10 (1969) 53-69 and 2276-2290. [read in part]
    (Antoine II in Lean, HilbertUnphysicality/FrameCutoffs.lean)
    See the file of the same name in ../QuantumVectorSpaces/.
    Antoine II, Sec. II, makes a symmetry group compatible with the space of states by requiring
    that space to be invariant under the group's representation. FrameCutoffs.lean shows that
    the group cannot be all smooth changes of frame: no nonzero state has finite position
    variance in every smooth frame both now and after a free evolution.
    PDF: roberts1966.pdf, antoine1969.pdf, antoine1969II.pdf

15. R. de la Madrid, The role of the rigged Hilbert space in quantum mechanics, Eur. J. Phys.
    26 (2005) 287-312, arXiv:quant-ph/0502053. [agent]
    The space of "physically preparable wave packets", "very similar to the Schwartz space".
    PDF: delaMadrid2005-quant-ph0502053.pdf

16. D. Buchholz, H. Grundling, The resolvent algebra, J. Funct. Anal. 254 (2008) 2725-2779,
    arXiv:0705.1988; D. Buchholz, The resolvent algebra: ideals and dimension, J. Funct. Anal.
    266 (2014) 3286-3302, arXiv:1307.6416, Theorem 3.7. [agent]
    [B]STDIST: the number of degrees of freedom is an isomorphism invariant of the algebra.
    PDF: BuchholzGrundling2008-0705.1988.pdf, Buchholz2014-1307.6416.pdf

17. M. Keyl, J. Kiukas, R. F. Werner, Schwartz operators, Rev. Math. Phys. 28 (2016) 1630001,
    arXiv:1503.04086. [agent, abstract]
    The density-operator analogue of Schwartz functions.
    PDF: KeylKiukasWerner2016-1503.04086.pdf

18. A. Winter, arXiv:1712.10267; M. E. Shirokov, arXiv:1806.05668. [agent, abstracts]
    [B]COBS inside the Hilbert-space model: energy-constrained norms.
    PDF: Winter2017-1712.10267.pdf, Shirokov2020-1806.05668.pdf

19. P. Zanardi, D. A. Lidar, S. Lloyd, Quantum tensor product structures are
    observable-induced, Phys. Rev. Lett. 92, 060402 (2004), arXiv:quant-ph/0308043. [agent,
    abstract]
    [B]STDIST: the structure that distinguishes systems lies in the observables.
    PDF: ZanardiLidarLloyd2004-quant-ph0308043.pdf
