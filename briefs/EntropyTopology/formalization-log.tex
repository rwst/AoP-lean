\section{Entropy is not continuous in standard probability spaces}

\paragraph{Files.}\leavevmode
\begin{itemize}
\item File: \lean{briefs/EntropyTopology/EntropyTopology.lean}
\item Library: \lean{EntropyTopology}
\item Imports: \lean{Mathlib.Analysis.SpecialFunctions.BinaryEntropy},\newline
  \lean{Mathlib.Analysis.SpecialFunctions.Log.Base},\newline
  \lean{Mathlib.MeasureTheory.Integral.Bochner.Set},\newline
  \lean{ForMathlib.Analysis.SpecialFunctions.Log.NegMulLog},\newline
  \lean{ForMathlib.MeasureTheory.Measure.TotalVariation.Basic}
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Setting.} A measure space $(X, \mu)$ (\lean{MeasureTheory.Measure}). The predicate
\lean{IsDensity} says that $\rho \colon X \to \mathbb{R}$ is measurable, pointwise
nonnegative, integrable with $\int \rho\, d\mu = 1$, and that $\rho \log \rho$ is integrable.
This is the space $P_\mu$; as a type it is \lean{Density}. The entropy $S$ and the
Jensen--Shannon divergence are in bits, and $d_1(\rho, \sigma) = \int |\rho - \sigma|\, d\mu$.
The probability measure $p_\rho$ with density $\rho$ is \lean{prob} $\mu\,\rho$, and the total
variation distance is Mathlib's \lean{tvDist}, i.e.\ $|p_\rho - p_\sigma|(X)$.

\paragraph{Differences from the brief.}
\begin{itemize}
\item Densities are functions, not $L^1$ classes, and are required to be measurable and
  nonnegative everywhere rather than almost everywhere. ``$S(\rho) \in \mathbb{R}$'' is read as
  integrability of $\rho\log\rho$.
\item Lemma~4 is proved through the binary entropy. With $p = (1+u)/2$ it says
  $(2p-1)^2/2 \le \ln 2 - H(p) \le \ln 2\,|2p-1|$. The lower bound comes from concavity of
  $H(p) + 2(p - \tfrac12)^2$, using $H'' = -1/(p(1-p)) \le -4$, which is the brief's
  second-derivative argument. The upper bound comes from concavity of $H$, the brief's chord
  argument.
\item Eq.~(18) uses Cauchy--Schwarz. The formal proof uses the pointwise inequality
  $|a-b| \le \frac{(a-b)^2}{(a+b)\,2c} + \frac{c(a+b)}{2}$ with $c = d_1/2$, which gives the same
  bound $d_1^2 \le 2\int (\rho-\sigma)^2/(\rho+\sigma)$.
\item Eq.~(12) and the topological statements are formalized through convergence and
  neighbourhoods. The ``topology generated by JSD balls'' is not constructed; JSD is not a metric.
\item Eq.~(2) is formalized in both readings of $\|\cdot\|_{\mathrm{TV}}$ (see the problem
  below). The norm reading is proved for any two integrable densities by a Hahn decomposition
  $\{\rho \le \sigma\}$; no $\sigma$-finiteness of $\mu$ is needed, which matters since
  Theorem~21 uses infinite $\mu$.
\item In Theorem~21 the hypothesis $1 < \mu(A_n)$ is dropped. Only $\mu(A_n) \to \infty$ is
  used, and all statements hold eventually in $n$. The first remark (counting measure) is not
  formalized separately; it is the special case $X$ countable.
\item Theorem~46 is proved in two forms. The main form assumes only that the \emph{equal}
  mixture $(\rho, \sigma) \mapsto (\rho+\sigma)/2$ is continuous. That is weaker than the
  brief's hypothesis that all mixing is continuous, so the formal statement is stronger. The
  \lean{_of_mixture} forms take the brief's hypothesis literally, reading ``statistical
  mixing (e.g.\ $(\lambda, \rho, \sigma) \mapsto \lambda\rho + (1-\lambda)\sigma$)'' as this
  map $[0, 1] \times P_\mu \times P_\mu \to P_\mu$ (\lean{Density.mixture}), with the product
  topology. They follow by fixing $\lambda = \tfrac12$.
\end{itemize}

\paragraph{Problems found in the brief.} No mathematical errors. One notational ambiguity:
\begin{itemize}
\item Eq.~(2), $\|p_\rho - p_\sigma\|_{\mathrm{TV}} = \tfrac12\|\rho - \sigma\|_{L^1(\mu)}$, does
  not say which total variation is meant. With the usual total variation norm
  $|p_\rho - p_\sigma|(X)$, which is Mathlib's \lean{tvDist}, the factor $\tfrac12$ is wrong:
  $|p_\rho - p_\sigma|(X) = \|\rho - \sigma\|_{L^1(\mu)}$ (\lean{tvDist_prob}). The factor is
  right for the distance $\sup_A |p_\rho(A) - p_\sigma(A)|$ (\lean{isGreatest_abs_prob_sub}).
  Nothing downstream depends on the constant, since only convergence is used.
\end{itemize}
Gaps the formalization had to fill:
\begin{itemize}
\item Prop.~10 and Theorem~46 use $(\rho+\sigma)/2 \in P_\mu$, i.e.\ that the mixture has finite
  entropy. The brief does not prove this. It follows from the pointwise bounds
  $0 \le \mathrm{JSD}\text{-integrand} \le \tfrac{\ln 2}{2}|\rho-\sigma|$
  (\lean{isDensity_mid}).
\item Theorem~46 presupposes that $P_\mu$ is closed under mixing, which the brief does not
  prove either. For $m = \lambda a + (1-\lambda) b$ and $\eta(x) = -x\ln x$, concavity and
  subadditivity of $\eta$ give
  $0 \le \eta(m) - \lambda\eta(a) - (1-\lambda)\eta(b) \le \eta(\lambda)\,a + \eta(1-\lambda)\,b$,
  so $\eta(m)$ is integrable (\lean{isDensity_mixture}).
\item In Theorem~21, $\varepsilon_n = 1/\log_2\mu(A_n) \in (0, 1)$ needs $\mu(A_n) > 2$, not
  $\mu(A_n) > 1$. The brief's ``passing to a tail'' covers this.
\end{itemize}
