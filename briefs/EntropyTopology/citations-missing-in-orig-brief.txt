Brief: Entropy is not continuous in standard probability spaces
Compiled 2026-11-14 from the literature search in ~/physics/plans/report-hilbert.html.

The brief has no reference list. Each entry below gives the work, the place in the brief it
bears on, what it says, and how well this was checked:
  [read]    the statement was read in the paper (the passage named, not the whole paper);
  [agent]   read by a search agent only, not re-read;
  [bib]     existence and bibliographic data confirmed, content not read.
PDF names refer to ~/physics/papers/. Entries marked (in Lean) are cited in the Lean files of
this directory.

The search was aimed at the quantum briefs, so only the quantum counterparts were found. The
classical literature on continuity of the Shannon and differential entropy in total variation
was not searched.

1. A. Wehrl, General properties of entropy, Rev. Mod. Phys. 50 (1978) 221-260, Sec. II.D.
   [read] (in Lean)
   Bears on: Theorem 21 and the remarks after it. For the von Neumann entropy:
   - lower semicontinuity in trace norm (Naudts 1969; Wehrl 1976);
   - "unboundedness in every neighborhood": every state has states of infinite entropy
     arbitrarily close, so the entropy is discontinuous;
   - the set of states with finite entropy is of first category;
   - if Tr e^(-beta H) is finite and the energies converge with the states, the entropies
     converge.
   PDF: wehrl1978.pdf

2. M. E. Shirokov, Entropy characteristics of subsets of states I, Izv. Math. 70 (2006)
   1265-1292, arXiv:quant-ph/0510073, Proposition 1. [agent] (in Lean)
   Bears on: Theorem 21; Theorem 46 (which topology makes the entropy continuous). The
   entropy is continuous on the states with Tr(H rho) <= h iff Tr e^(-beta H) is finite for
   all beta > 0.
   PDF: Shirokov2006-quant-ph0510073.pdf

3. M. E. Shirokov, Continuity of the von Neumann entropy, Commun. Math. Phys. 296 (2010)
   625-654, arXiv:0904.1963. [agent]
   Bears on: Theorem 46. Conditions for continuity; credits the condition above to Wehrl.
   PDF: Shirokov2010-0904.1963.pdf

4. A. Winter, Energy-constrained diamond norm ..., arXiv:1712.10267; S. Becker, N. Datta,
   Commun. Math. Phys. (2020), arXiv:1810.00863. [agent, abstract / bib]
   Bears on: Theorem 46. Continuity bounds under an energy constraint.
   PDF: Winter2017-1712.10267.pdf, BeckerDatta2020-1810.00863.pdf
