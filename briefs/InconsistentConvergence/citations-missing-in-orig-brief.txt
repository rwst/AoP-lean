Brief: Inconsistent convergence in Hilbert space
Compiled 2026-11-14 from a literature search.

The brief has no reference list. Each entry below gives the work, the place in the brief it
bears on, what it says, and how well this was checked:
  [read]    the statement was read in the paper (the passage named, not the whole paper);
  [agent]   read by a search agent only, not re-read;
  [bib]     existence and bibliographic data confirmed, content not read.
PDF names refer to ~/physics/papers/. Entries marked (in Lean) are cited in the Lean files of
this directory.

1. G. Carcassi, F. Calderón, C. A. Aidala, The unphysicality of Hilbert spaces, Quantum Stud.:
   Math. Found. 12, 13 (2025), arXiv:2308.06669. [agent] (in Lean)
   Bears on: the whole brief.
   PDF: CarcassiCalderonAidala2023-2308.06669.pdf

2. K. Schmüdgen, Unbounded Self-adjoint Operators on Hilbert Space, GTM 265 (2012),
   Proposition 10.1; B. Simon, arXiv:1711.00528, Theorem 10.14 (Davies-Kato). [agent]
   (Schmüdgen in Lean)
   Bears on: Sec. 3, eq. (4)-(5). A lower semibounded form is closed iff it is lower
   semicontinuous. So the expected number can jump up in the limit, as in the brief, but
   never down.
   PDF: Schmüdgen-Book.pdf, Simon2018-1711.00528.pdf

3. A. Wehrl, General properties of entropy, Rev. Mod. Phys. 50 (1978) 221-260, Sec. II.D.
   [read] (in Lean)
   Bears on: Sec. 3-4. The same phenomenon for the entropy, with the remark that these
   discontinuities "do not really affect physics" when the energy is controlled.
   PDF: wehrl1978.pdf

4. J. E. Roberts, J. Math. Phys. 7 (1966) 1097-1104, Theorem 1; Commun. Math. Phys. 3 (1966)
   98-119, Proposition 11. [read] (in Lean, SchwartzObservables)
   Bears on: Sec. 3 last sentence, Sec. 4 ("a topology that keeps track of all moments"). The
   coarsest topology that makes a given set of observables continuous on their maximal
   invariant domain.
   PDF: roberts1966dirac.pdf, roberts1966.pdf

5. J.-P. Antoine, J. Math. Phys. 10 (1969) 53-69. [read: abstract, introduction] (in Lean,
   SchwartzObservables)
   Bears on: Sec. 4. Unbounded operators made continuous by a finer topology; credits
   Kristensen, Mejlbo, Thue Poulsen (1965).
   PDF: antoine1969.pdf

6. R. Werner, Found. Phys. 13 (1983) 859-881. [read in part]
   Bears on: Sec. 4, "a meaningful criterion of physical convergence". Physical uniformities
   on quantum states, for bounded observables; the opposite conclusion (a coarser topology).
   PDF: werner1983.pdf

7. A. Winter, arXiv:1712.10267; M. E. Shirokov, arXiv:1806.05668. [agent, abstracts]
   Bears on: Sec. 4. Convergence with an energy constraint.
   PDF: Winter2017-1712.10267.pdf, Shirokov2020-1806.05668.pdf

8. Z. Lu, arXiv:2603.10601. [agent]
   Bears on: Sec. 1, Sec. 4. Argues the discontinuity is not a physical problem.
   PDF: Lu2026-2603.10601.pdf
