\noindent
Open problems raised by the technical brief in this directory, and by its Lean formalization
(see \lean{formalization-log.tex}).
Items marked \textsc{[blocks verification]} stand in the way of checking a main
claim of the brief as the brief states it: a \emph{formalization gap} can be closed
in Lean alone, while a \emph{brief issue} needs the brief to be corrected or made
precise first.

No item below blocks the verification of a main claim of the brief.

\section*{From the brief}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Infinite entropy.} EQST takes the entropy to be real-valued, but in infinite
  dimension the von Neumann entropy of some density operators, and the Shannon entropy of some
  countable families of weights, is $+\infty$. With real values BR-ENT and [B]MUTEX cannot hold
  in infinite dimension. Which codomain is intended, and whether [B]MUTEX should be restricted to
  ensembles of finite entropy, is open. The formalization uses $[0, \infty]$. (Brief, Sec.~3,
  EQST; Log, ``Problems found'', first item.)
\item \textbf{Independence of the conditions.} The conclusion says that the only extra requirement
  is the consistency [B]MUTEX. Whether BR-BORN and BR-ENT are each independent of the other over
  EQST (without [B]MUTEX), that is, whether there are a $p$ satisfying EQST and BR-BORN and an $S$
  violating [B]MUTEX, and conversely, is not addressed. (Brief, Sec.~4.)
\end{enumerate}

\section*{From the formalization}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Countable affinity and Corollary~8.} EQST is formalized with affinity for mixtures of
  two pure states only. The brief's affinity for countable mixtures, and Corollary~8 (that $p$ is
  determined by its values on pure states, which in infinite dimension needs countable
  mixtures and so countable affinity), are not formalized. (Log, ``Differences'', third item.)
\item \textbf{The Bloch-ball picture.} The geometric diagonalization of Fig.~1 and eqs.~(4)--(5),
  and the picture of $p(\psi|\cdot)$ as an affine function on the Bloch ball of Fig.~3, are not
  formalized; the proofs are algebraic instead. (Log, ``Differences'', last item.)
\end{enumerate}
