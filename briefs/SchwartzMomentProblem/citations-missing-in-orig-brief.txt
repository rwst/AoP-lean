Brief: Non-uniqueness of the moment problem for Schwartz functions
Compiled 2026-11-14 from a literature search.

The brief has no reference list. Each entry below gives the work, the place in the brief it
bears on, what it says, and how well this was checked:
  [read]    the statement was read in the paper (the passage named, not the whole paper);
  [agent]   read by a search agent only, not re-read;
  [bib]     existence and bibliographic data confirmed, content not read.
PDF names refer to ~/physics/papers/. Entries marked (in Lean) are cited in the Lean files of
this directory.

1. S. L. Woronowicz, The quantum problem of moments I, Rep. Math. Phys. 1 (1970) 135. [read]
   (in Lean)
   Bears on: Theorem 1 and its proof; Sec. 3 (locality).
   - Counterexample II (final form credited to J. Plebański): for normalized Schwartz
     functions phi_1, phi_2 with disjoint supports, |phi_1 + phi_2><phi_1 + phi_2| and
     |phi_1><phi_1| + |phi_2><phi_2| give the same functional on the algebra generated by q
     and p, since "the supports of functions are never enlarged by differential operators".
     The brief's Theorem 1 varies a relative phase instead; the mechanism is the same.
   - Counterexample I: not every positive functional on that algebra comes from a density
     operator.
   - Main theorem: if sum_n sqrt(omega(a^n (a+)^n)) t^n / n! is finite for some t > 0, exactly
     one density operator with range in S represents omega. The condition says the state
     vector is an analytic vector for the creation operator. This is a sufficient condition
     for the question "which states are determined by their moments" (Sec. 1).
   PDF: woronowicz1970.pdf

2. S. L. Woronowicz, The quantum problem of moments II, Rep. Math. Phys. 1 (1971) 175. [read:
   statements] (in Lean)
   Bears on: Sec. 1, Sec. 3. Theorem 1: a functional on the algebra is Tr(x rho) iff it is
   non-negative on every positive element. Theorem 6: when rho is unique. On the larger
   algebra of all operators on S, rho always exists and is unique.
   PDF: woronowicz1971.pdf

3. M. Keyl, J. Kiukas, R. F. Werner, Schwartz operators, Rev. Math. Phys. 28 (2016) 1630001,
   arXiv:1503.04086. [agent, abstract] (in Lean)
   Bears on: Sec. 1. Operator moment problems: is T determined by Tr(Q^a P^b T)?
   PDF: KeylKiukasWerner2016-1503.04086.pdf

4. G. Lassner, W. Timmermann, Normal states on algebras of unbounded operators, Rep. Math.
   Phys. 3 (1972) 295-305. [read: introduction]
   Bears on: Sec. 1. Context for Woronowicz's results.
   PDF: LassnerTimmermann1972.pdf

5. T. Thrien, G. Carcassi, D. Carfì, C. A. Aidala, Quantum tomography on Schwartz space,

   Bears on: Sec. 3. What does determine a Schwartz state: Wigner function, quadratures.
