\noindent
Open problems raised by the technical brief in this directory, and by its Lean formalization
(see \lean{formalization-log.tex}).

\section*{From the brief}
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Time-dependent evolution.} The equivalence of DR-SCEQ, DR-UNIT, DR-EV, DR-PROB and
  DR-INFO without EVTI, for two-parameter families $\Phi_{t_0 \to t_1}$ and a time-dependent
  Hamiltonian $H(t)$. The brief only says ``We are going to restrict the dynamics to the
  time-independent case''. That case is formalized as the brief writes it, with the two-parameter
  [B]EVMIX, EVTI, and DR-SCEQ with $H(t)$ (\lean{TwoParameter.schrodinger_tfae}). Without EVTI
  there is no one-parameter group, and DR-UNIT, DR-EV, DR-PROB and DR-INFO would have to be
  restated for the propagators $\Phi_{t_0 \to t_1}$. (Brief, Sec.~3, before Condition EVTI, and
  Condition DR-SCEQ; log, ``The conditions as the brief writes them''.)
\item \textbf{Isolation as determinism and reversibility.} ``Conceptually, all these are
  equivalent to saying that the system is isolated, as future and past states are determined,
  and only determined, by the present state of the system.'' Isolation is not defined, so this
  equivalence is not stated as a theorem. (Brief, Sec.~6.)
\end{enumerate}

\section*{From the formalization}
\noindent Beyond finite dimension the formalization has a part in any dimension, which covers
unbounded $H$; it leaves open the item ``Shift semigroup counterexample'' below.
\begin{enumerate}
\item \textbf{Shift semigroup counterexample.} The right shifts $V_t$ on $L^2(0,\infty)$ give an
  evolution $\Phi_t(\rho) = V_t\rho V_t^\dagger$ that maps pure states to pure states and
  preserves the Born rule and the entropy, but satisfies neither DR-EV nor DR-UNIT. It shows
  that the reconstructed past clauses of DR-PROB and DR-INFO are needed in infinite dimension.
  Formalized only as a single map (\lean{shift_preserves_not_unitary}), not as a semigroup
  satisfying \lean{IsEvolution}. (Log, ``Hilbert spaces of any dimension'', ``Problems found in
  the brief''.)
\item \textbf{Proposition~1 for equal mixtures.} Proposition~1 is proved for maps affine under
  all convex combinations. A version assuming only $T(\tfrac12\rho + \tfrac12\sigma) =
  \tfrac12T\rho + \tfrac12T\sigma$ ``would need continuity first, to pass from dyadic weights to
  all weights''; not formalized. (Log, ``Hilbert spaces of any dimension'', ``Differences from
  the brief''.)
\end{enumerate}
